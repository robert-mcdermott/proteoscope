Scene

Compare structures

Tick Add to the scene in the Structure tab and open a second structure to superpose it, or compare an experimental structure with its AlphaFold model.

Density map

The 2Fo-Fc and Fo-Fc maps of an X-ray entry or the map of a cryo-EM entry, from the PDBe volume server (network); or a CCP4/MRC map file, read on this computer.

Validation

The wwPDB validation report of a PDB entry: clashes, geometry, Ramachandran and rotamer outliers, and fit to the density. Requires network access.

Interactions

Double-click a ligand or residue (or select it and press F) to focus it. Non-covalent contacts to its surroundings are detected and drawn.

Solvent accessibility

Shrake-Rupley SASA (1.4 Å probe). Also enables the solvent-exposure color scheme and buried surface area for chain pairs.

Ramachandran plot

Per-residue profile

Conservation

Residue conservation from a multiple sequence alignment: the MSA of an opened prediction, or an alignment file whose first sequence is the protein (A3M, aligned FASTA, Stockholm or Clustal). Jensen–Shannon divergence (Capra & Singh 2007) in ConSurf-style grades.

Predicted aligned error

Fetch an AlphaFold DB entry, open a PAE JSON (AlphaFold DB, AlphaFold 3, ColabFold) or a prediction folder to see the PAE matrix. Drag on the plot to highlight residue ranges.

Everything here runs locally in your browser. Proteomics data is never uploaded.

Search results

Public evidence

Peptides observed in PeptideAtlas, ProteomicsDB and other resources, and modification sites from reprocessed PRIDE datasets (EBI Proteins API, PTMeXchange). Requires network access.

Sequence properties

ExPASy ProtParam conventions: average masses, Bjellqvist pK set, Pace extinction coefficients.

UniProt annotations

Curated domains, sites, PTMs and variants from UniProtKB, mapped to this structure through its UniProt cross-reference (SIFTS/DBREF). Requires network access.

Cross-links (XL-MS)

Enter links, or open a xiFDR, xiVIEW, pLink, MeroX, XlinkX, MS Annika or MaxLynx export (or drop it on the window); proteins are matched by chain, UniProt accession or name.

HDX-MS

DynamX (state or cluster data), HDExaminer (all results or uptake summary) or an HDX data table (Masson et al. 2019). Peptides are matched to the chain sequences; differences are tested with the hybrid test when replicates are known.
