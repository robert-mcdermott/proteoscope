Sessions, figures and methods

Export images, videos and tables, save the whole workspace as a session, share a view as a link or in Mol*, and draft a methods paragraph for what you did.

Images and spin videos

Export image (the camera button in the toolbar, or P) renders the current view off screen as a PNG. You can choose:

  • Resolution: 1× to 4× of the viewport, optionally supersampled for smooth edges.
  • What to include: optionally a transparent background, the color legend, labels and measurements.

Copy puts the image on the clipboard. Spin video records one full rotation of the view as WebM. The png command exports an image from the search box; see Scripting and command line for exporting from a script.

Tables as CSV

Interaction tables, docking scores and fingerprints, prediction rankings, and proteomics sites with their statistics export as CSV, for a spreadsheet or your own analysis.

TableWhat the CSV holdsMore
InteractionsThe interaction tableViewing and interactions
Docking posesThe scores and interaction fingerprints of every poseLigands and docking
Prediction rankingEvery model and chain pair with its scoresPredictions and AlphaFold
Proteomics sitesEvery localized site with its probability, value, part-sphere exposure and whether it lies in a disordered region; after a differential test, also each site's fold change, p, q and degrees of freedom, and for adjusted sites the protein's changeProteomics

Sessions

A session saves the whole workspace so you can reopen it later. Save session (Structure tab, or the save command) writes a .proteoscope.json file. To reopen it, open it like any file, or drop it on the window.

What a session records

  • Where each structure came from: a fetched PDB ID or UniProt accession, a bundled example, or the local file itself, embedded and compressed.
  • How it looks: styles, per-residue styling and colors, surfaces, superpositions (refitted when the session opens), ensemble overlays and trimming.
  • What you marked: selections, focus, labels, proteomics overlays and measurements.
  • The view: the camera, lighting, background, clipping and secondary-structure source.
  • Prediction data: PAE matrices and contact probabilities you opened (stored at 0.125 Å and 1/255 steps), prediction scores, and MSA depth.
  • Docking and maps: docking poses, with their files embedded, and the density map's settings.

What is fetched or computed again

Some results are not stored in the file. When the session opens:

  • solvent accessibility, PAE domains, validation reports and AlphaMissense are recomputed or fetched again;
  • AlphaFold DB PAE matrices are fetched again;
  • maps from the server are fetched again.

Map files. A map you opened from a file on your computer is not stored in the session. Open the map file again after opening the session.

Share a link

Copy link (or the link command) puts the whole session in the URL, as #session=…. This works when every structure was fetched or is a bundled example; when a structure came from a local file, save a session instead.

The link opens in Proteoscope at the same address and port. To open a structure from a link without a session, use a #fetch= link; see Links that open structures.

Open the view in Mol*

MolViewSpec (or the mvs command) exports the view for the Mol* viewer. Drop the exported file onto the Mol* viewer to open it.

FileWhenContents
.mvsjNo structure is a local fileThe view; the structures are downloaded from RCSB PDB and AlphaFold DB
.mvsxSome structures are local filesAn archive with the view and the files inside

The export carries representations, per-residue colors, sticks, surfaces, labels, superposition transforms, the camera and the background. Proteoscope's lighting effects are not part of the format.

Methods paragraph

Methods (Structure tab, next to Save session) drafts a methods paragraph from what the session used:

  • each structure's source with its ID, experimental method, resolution and revision date, or the model's database version;
  • each analysis with its method and parameters: superposition, interactions, surfaces, density fit, conservation, statistics and others;
  • the Proteoscope version.

Citations are numbered, with DOIs checked against Crossref. Copy references or Download BibTeX takes them to your manuscript.

Check the draft. The paragraph is a starting point. Check the text against what you did before using it.

Citing Proteoscope

To cite Proteoscope itself, use its CITATION.cff file; GitHub offers it under Cite this repository on the repository page. See Cite Proteoscope for details.
